# Proposal

## Why

The LLM Wiki landing and lesson 1 state that the index-first method works "bez embeddings i RAG do ~500 źródeł". The figure has never been measured. The owner has decided that no promotion of the course relies on it, yet it is live on the page every post and newsletter link points to (`/llm-wiki`) and on the lesson that follows the signup (`/llm-wiki/kurs/1-zaloz-katalog`). It also sits in `public/llms-full.txt`, which answers AI crawlers and assistants with the same number. Removing it is due by 2026-10-16 (PL-462).

## What Changes

- The "Index-first" thesis on the landing drops the sentence "Do ~500 źródeł bez embeddings i RAG." and keeps what the method does: the agent reads the index and opens only the matching notes.
- Lesson 1 loses the threshold in three places: the closing sentence of the progressive-disclosure section, the answer to "Czym LLM Wiki różni się od RAG i baz wektorowych?", and the FAQ entry "Co, gdy baza urośnie powyżej ~500 źródeł?".
- That FAQ entry is **rewritten without a threshold**, not deleted: the reader's question stands, and the two options it names (split into topic sub-bases, or add a search layer) are existing course content. Its scaling assurance ("skaluje się dalej", "granica ~500 … nie twardy limit") goes, because without the figure it would be an unbounded promise instead of a bounded one.
- The lesson's `updated` date and the landing's `@sitemapUpdated` marker move to the change date, as both folders require when content changes.
- `public/llms-full.txt` and `public/sitemap.xml` are regenerated by the repository scripts, not edited by hand.
- A new requirement records the rule so the figure does not return: course copy states no scale limit it has not measured.

No new number, claim, or promise replaces the old one. Nothing outside these three passages changes; in particular the heading "Dlaczego to działa bez RAG" and the intro line that says the base works "bez embeddings i bez RAG" are untouched.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `course-content-style`: gains a requirement that course and landing copy name no scale limit for the method unless it is backed by a measurement. Today the spec governs vocabulary and definitions only, so nothing stopped an unmeasured figure from being written into a lesson.

## Impact

- **Copy (PL only; the course and the landing have no English version):** `src/pages/LlmWikiLanding.jsx` (one string, plus the `@sitemapUpdated` marker), `src/content/kurs/1-zaloz-katalog.md` (three passages plus `updated`).
- **Generated, tracked:** `public/llms-full.txt`, `public/sitemap.xml`. `public/llms.txt` is regenerated and expected to stay unchanged.
- **Generated, ignored:** `src/data/generated/` is rebuilt locally for verification and is not committed.
- **Tests:** the existing `llm-wiki-landing`, `llm-wiki-course` and `llm-wiki-discoverable` specs cover these surfaces; none asserts the removed wording, so no test changes. The lesson-1 FAQPage JSON-LD is generated from the lesson, so the rewritten entry flows through on its own.
- **Docs:** `src/content/kurs/AGENTS.md` gains one Work Guidance bullet that records the trap and points to the new requirement. The rule itself lives only in the spec. No other `AGENTS.md` changes.
- **Not touched:** blog articles (the "~500 words" hits there describe note length, not a knowledge limit), `docs/blog/_archive`, other lessons, and the hub FAQ in `src/data/courseFaq.js`.
- **After merge:** confirm live that `/llm-wiki`, `/llm-wiki/kurs/1-zaloz-katalog` and `/llms-full.txt` contain no "500 źr".
