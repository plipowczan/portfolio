# Design

## Context

See `proposal.md` for why. The figure is in three tracked sources and two kinds of derived file:

- `src/pages/LlmWikiLanding.jsx` — one string in `VALUE_INDEX`.
- `src/content/kurs/1-zaloz-katalog.md` — a closing sentence, a FAQ answer, and a whole FAQ entry.
- `public/llms-full.txt` — tracked and generated by `scripts/generate-llms-txt.js`; it repeats lesson 1 verbatim.
- `src/data/generated/` — generated by `scripts/generate-content.mjs` and git-ignored, so it is rebuilt for local verification and never committed.

The lesson's FAQ is read twice at build time: rendered as markdown, and turned into `FAQPage` JSON-LD by the FAQ extractor. A rewritten entry therefore reaches the structured data without a code change.

The folder rules that apply: lessons follow `.claude/rules/content/10-prosty-polski.md`; a content change moves the lesson's `updated` (`src/content/kurs/AGENTS.md`) and the landing's `@sitemapUpdated` marker (`src/AGENTS.md`), because the sitemap `<lastmod>` is read from them.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Remove the figure and every sentence whose only support was the figure.
- Introduce no replacement number, claim, or assurance.
- Keep the change reviewable in one sitting: three old/new pairs.

**Non-Goals:**

- Re-examining whether the method works without RAG at all. "Działa bez embeddings i bez RAG" appears without a figure in the lesson intro and in the heading "Dlaczego to działa bez RAG"; both stay as they are.
- Touching other lessons, the hub FAQ, or blog articles.
- Building an automated gate for the figure. The rule is recorded in the spec; the grep in the tasks is a one-off check.

## Decisions

### D1. Delete the figure; do not soften it

"Do ~500 źródeł" is replaced by nothing, not by "do kilkuset źródeł" or "do średniej wielkości bazy". Each of those is the same claim with a blurrier number, and the owner's decision is that no scale claim is made.

### D2. Where deleting the figure would strengthen the claim, change the verb

Three passages carry the figure; the first two lose it cleanly and the third needs one word changed:

| Place | Old | New |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing, thesis "Index-first" | Agent czyta indeks i wchodzi tylko w trafne noty. Do ~500 źródeł bez embeddings i RAG. | Agent czyta indeks i wchodzi tylko w trafne noty. Bez embeddings i RAG. |
| Lesson 1, end of "Dlaczego to działa bez RAG" | To dlatego baza działa **bez embeddings i bez RAG do ~500 źródeł**: nie trzeba liczyć wektorów, gdy dobry indeks pozwala agentowi zawęzić wyszukiwanie samą lekturą. | To dlatego baza działa **bez embeddings i bez RAG**: nie trzeba liczyć wektorów, gdy dobry indeks pozwala agentowi zawęzić wyszukiwanie samą lekturą. |
| Lesson 1, FAQ "Czym LLM Wiki różni się od RAG i baz wektorowych?" (last sentence) | Nie liczy wektorów ani embeddingów - do ~500 źródeł dobry indeks wystarcza, żeby zawęzić wyszukiwanie samą lekturą. | Nie liczy wektorów ani embeddingów - dobry indeks pozwala zawęzić wyszukiwanie samą lekturą. |

In the third row "wystarcza" becomes "pozwala". With the bound gone, "dobry indeks wystarcza" would read as sufficiency at any size, which is a stronger claim than the one being removed. "Pozwala zawęzić" is the wording the lesson already uses one section earlier, so the lesson stays consistent with itself.

The landing keeps "Bez embeddings i RAG." as a description of the method. This is the one place the reviewer may prefer to cut further (see Open Questions).

### D3. Rewrite the "baza urośnie" FAQ entry; do not delete it

The question is a real objection a reader has after learning the method, and the FAQ is built from objections. Deleting it would leave the lesson silent on the obvious next question. The answer is rewritten to use only text the entry already contained:

| | Old | New |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question | Co, gdy baza urośnie powyżej ~500 źródeł? | Co, gdy baza bardzo urośnie? |
| Answer | Progressive disclosure skaluje się dalej - indeks i graf rosną wolniej niż treść, więc agent nadal zawęża zanim otworzy notę. Przy bardzo dużych bazach można podzielić wiedzę na tematyczne podbazy (multi-brain, lekcja 5) albo dołożyć warstwę wyszukiwania. Granica ~500 to moment, w którym warto o tym pomyśleć, a nie twardy limit. | Przy bardzo dużych bazach można podzielić wiedzę na tematyczne podbazy (multi-brain, lekcja 5) albo dołożyć warstwę wyszukiwania. |

Cut from the answer: "skaluje się dalej … agent nadal zawęża" and "indeks i graf rosną wolniej niż treść" — an unmeasured scaling assurance that only made sense as a continuation of "past 500" — and the closing sentence about the boundary. Kept: the sentence that names the two options, verbatim. No sentence is added, so nothing new needs to be defended. The question wording reuses "bardzo dużych bazach" from the answer.

Alternative considered: keep "skaluje się dalej" and drop only the number. Rejected: without the threshold it promises unbounded scaling.

### D4. Regenerate, then diff

`public/llms-full.txt` and `public/sitemap.xml` are produced by `node scripts/generate-llms-txt.js` and `npm run blog:sitemap`. They are regenerated after the source edits and the diff is read: it must contain only the three lesson passages in `llms-full.txt` and the two `<lastmod>` lines in `sitemap.xml`. Unrelated drift in either file is a signal to stop, not to commit it.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The landing still says "Bez embeddings i RAG"] → It is a description of the method, with no scale attached, and the lesson intro already says the same. Flagged to the reviewer rather than decided silently.
- [The shortened FAQ answer is thin] → Accepted. It is shorter because the rest was the retired assurance; padding it would invent content.
- [Regeneration pulls in drift from other merged changes] → The diff in D4 is read before commit; any unrelated hunk is reported, not committed.
- [`updated` / `@sitemapUpdated` bump signals a fresher page to crawlers] → Intended: the copy changed, and the repository rule is to move the date with it.

## Open Questions

- Whether the owner wants the landing to drop "Bez embeddings i RAG." as well, leaving only "Agent czyta indeks i wchodzi tylko w trafne noty." This is a copy choice for the PR review and does not change the spec, the approach, or the tasks beyond one string.
